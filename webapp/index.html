🏙️ Set up your apartment alerts

Fill in what you're looking for, then download your settings. Everything stays in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

How this works — this page just builds your settings file; it does not send alerts by itself. To get a daily email of matching apartments:
  1. Fill in the form below and click Download settings.
  2. Get your own free copy of the app from the GitHub repository and drop the settings file into it.
  3. Add your email address, email app-password, and a free RentCast API key as secrets, then turn on the daily job (GitHub Actions).
Full step-by-step instructions are in the repository's README. You run your own copy — your details never leave your machine.
🔒 No passwords here. This page only saves your search preferences. Your email password and any API key are added separately during setup — see the guide.
